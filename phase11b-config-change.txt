Phase 11B discovery correction

Before:
  hmd_presence = ["driver_bigscreen_desktop.dll"]

After:
  hmd_presence = ["*.*"]

Reason:
  Valve's OpenVR manifest contract defines hmd_presence as VID.PID patterns;
  *.* is the documented always-present pattern. The previous value caused the
  client-side HmdNotFound gate before vrserver loaded the driver.

No SteamVR user setting, Valve driver, SteamVR binary, or Virtual Display
source was changed.
